# Product

**What:** the public website of the Gymnázium Suverénního řádu maltézských rytířů ve Skutči, a school of the Czech Grand Priory of the Order of Malta. Two programmes ending in maturita: gymnázium (eight- and four-year) and bezpečnostně právní činnost. The school also runs a dormitory (domov mládeže) and a canteen.

**Who:** applicants and their parents (admissions, programmes, open days); current pupils and parents (schedule, substitutions, forms, canteen, Bakaláři); staff publishing news; the founder and the Grand Priory (identity, presentation to the Grand Prior).

**Register:** institutional but welcoming. It belongs to the family of the Grand Priory's sites (maltezskyrad.cz, lurdy.eu, velkoprevorskypalac.eu): same frame, type and colours, with a warmer, photo-led homepage, more open spacing and cards for news.

**Constraints:** Czech only; static site on Vercel; WCAG 2.2 AA; no cookies or tracking; Grand Magistry communications policy and coat-of-arms guidelines; no invented facts; the old site's URLs are preserved.
